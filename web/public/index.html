HOW TO PLAY

The mission: you're a googly in a flying saucer. Every planet in the universe belongs to the local googlies — until you cover it in paint. Paint 70% of a planet's surface and it's yours. There are 10 galaxies, each with 5 star systems of 3–5 planets, and the last planet of every system is guarded by a Guardian you have to beat too.

Flying: W A S D fly, the mouse steers and aims, Space climbs, C dives, Shift boosts.the stick flies, drag on the right to steer and aim, ▲ ▼ climb and dive, BOOST goes fast. Your UFO always hovers above the planet — you can't crash into it.

Painting: Left clickPAINT shoots paint blobs where the crosshair points (they fall in a curve, like real paint). Each shot uses paint from your tank; it refills by itself. Right clickBOMB drops a giant paint bomb once you buy it. Fill the Rainbow Nuke meter by painting, then press XNUKE.

The defenders: flak turrets shoot at you from the ground, fighter jets chase you, scrubbers drive around washing your paint off, storms rain it away, and shield domes block paint until you break them. Paint hurts all of them. Blasted defenders drop coins — fly close to grab them.

Money: you earn credits for every % painted, every defender, every coin, every googly you abduct with the tractor beam (hold EBEAM over them), and a big bonus for each planet. Spend them at the mothership on upgrades: faster and bigger paint, more barrels, a bigger tank, armour, shields, engines, bombs, drones that paint for you, an auto-turret and the nuke.

With friends: the UFO has 4 seats. The pilot flies; the other three are gunners who aim their own paint cannons. There are no computer crew — the seats are for real people online. Your progress saves by itself every 30 seconds, and you can leave a planet and come back later: your paint stays.
